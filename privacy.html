Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · applies to Ember Line, Bangalore Office Run and any other game published by Flowpoint Consulting FZ-LLC

The short version

Our games collect no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Flowpoint Consulting FZ-LLC ("we") is a company licensed by the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone, United Arab Emirates. We publish the games listed on this site and are responsible for this policy.

Uninstalling a game, or clearing its storage or your browser's site data, deletes this information. We cannot see it and cannot recover it.

Information we collect

None. The games do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and they do not use advertising or device identifiers.

Permissions

The Android apps use vibration for haptic feedback. They do not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other sensitive permission. The Android share sheet is opened only when you tap a share button, and you choose what to share and with whom.

Web versions

When you play a game in a web browser, your browser downloads the game and its fonts and code libraries from content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts and jsDelivr). Like any website, those services receive your IP address to deliver the files. We do not receive or store it.

Children

Because the games collect no personal information, they collect none from children.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when the conversation is finished, unless we need to keep them for legal reasons.

Changes

If a future version of a game adds anything that collects data (for example ads or online leaderboards), we will update this policy before that version is released, with a new effective date at the top of this page.
